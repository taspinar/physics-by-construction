# Dataset feasibility: registry, gate, and decisions

Real measurements are inputs that no build can regenerate (ADR 007). This folder holds the evidence for them. A dataset is not content until it has a **card** (`data/registry/<dataset>.yaml`), and it is not a lab until it has passed the **feasibility gate**: an actual small file inspected, every artifact labelled, one reference figure generated by source-controlled code, the rights read from the record, the sizes estimated, and a dossier written with a decision. The author's guide is "Measured data" in `docs/authoring.md`.

No spiked dataset has failed its gate so far. The SDSS Balmer spectrum was first recorded `defer`, because the SDSS-I/II page of SDSS Classic states a non-commercial approval clause that contradicts the current SDSS policy; the owner decided on 2026-11-09 that the current policy, which calls all publicly released SDSS data public domain, governs, and the card records `go`. Defer and reject have no entry. The survey-only cards with a known obstacle are the optical-diffraction record (licence field CC BY 4.0 beside a copyright line, RAR archives) and the cylinder-wake PIV (one 1.1 GB MAT file of processed fields at 20 Hz, zeros are masks); both remain conditional, as the survey proposed.

**Licences and readers since 2026-11-08.** The addendum of 2026-11-08 to [ADR 007](../decisions/007-real-datasets-as-declared-inputs.md) (Issue #417) counts Apache-2.0 and CC BY-SA as recorded equivalent permission for a committed sample, a ShareAlike sample and its figures carrying CC BY-SA on the page and on About; NC, ND, or unstated licences are still not committed. The first sample under Apache-2.0, the Xu damped pendulum, records its licence as `equivalent-permission` and names Apache-2.0 in each rights answer. MAT files are read with `scipy.io.loadmat` (`h5py` only for v7.3); XLSX, FITS, and CIF files are converted once, outside the build, by a checksum-pinned script that the card and the dossier record.

A `go` means the data, the rights, and the figure passed. It does not mean a lesson exists, and it does not pick a course.

## Dataset research for the measured-data labs

The research of 2026-11-08 into open datasets for the measured-data labs is recorded in [spike-candidates-2026-11-08](spike-candidates-2026-11-08.md): the first spike recommended per lab, the options rejected, corrections to existing cards (not yet applied), the owner's decisions and the open ones, and the outcome of its third round, in which the small spike files were downloaded and inspected. Those files, the scripts, and the figures were kept outside the repository; the research committed no data file. The datasets it marked ready for a dossier receive their card, sample, and decision in their own features, judged per dossier: a dataset has a `go` when its card says so (the tables above). AST8's FITS file is converted once by `tools/datasets/tess_lc_to_csv.py`, T7's sample is extracted from DWD's yearly archive by `tools/datasets/dwd_soil_extract.py`, Q7's FITS file by `tools/datasets/sdss_spec_to_csv.py`, T8's 40 frames are tracked once by `tools/datasets/bulk_water_tracks.py`, the project's own processing, disclosed as such; the build repeats it on the green channel of the three frames it commits (as a NumPy array: no image file is committed outside `site/assets/`), W7's two XLSX profiles and two morphometry files by `tools/datasets/lycopodium_xlsx_to_csv.py` and `tools/datasets/lycopodium_morphometry_to_utf8.py`, FL7's samples are extracted from the 1.1 GB PIV file and the ten hot-wire runs by `tools/datasets/cylinder_wake_extract.py` and `tools/datasets/ost_hotwire_extract.py`, FL10's ETOPO crop by `tools/datasets/etopo_ascii_to_npy.py`, and MAT5's 20 CIFs by `tools/datasets/srm660c_cif_to_csv.py` (`tools/datasets/README.md`).

## Recommendation for the first lab

**First: the GW150914 strain lab**, as the planner's recommendation in `docs/architecture.md` expected. The data are a 2.5 MB committed sample under CC BY 4.0, the figure reproduces from it in 4 s with no new dependency, the signal is a real, well-known event, and a consistency check against the published chirp mass and the detector delay is part of the lab. The cost the spike found: the lab needs whitening and spectral estimation, background that the learning path does not yet build, and a leading-order template recovers the mass only to about 15 %.

**Fallback: the NPL S-parameter lab.** The sample is 230 KB, the checks (passivity, reciprocity, the order of the pads, two instruments agreeing) are exact, and the background is lighter: complex numbers, transmission lines, decibels. Its cost is that calibration uncertainty is in a paper this spike did not read.

**Measured against modelled, later: the Aalborg flume**, which is the only dataset with a model comparison and residuals, once the source paper's gauge positions and wave parameters are read. Every lesson needs a primary course (ADR 008, invariant I18); which course hosts the first lab is a decision for the human and for F15, not for this spike.

## Evidence this spike left

- Figures regenerate with `uv run python -m pbc.data.reference <dataset> --out figure.png`; the status label and caption are in each dossier, for a reviewer to confirm.
- `uv run python -m pbc.data.check` is the dataset check of `./scripts/verify.sh`.
- `./scripts/fetch-data.sh <dataset>` downloads a full dataset for a learner.
